## Features the owner asked for

In the owner's order of interest:

1. **Dark mode** for FontLab 7's interface (first): running, [DARKMODE.md](DARKMODE.md).
2. Automated kerning: Latin, Armenian, Georgian, and kana pairs, kerning classes, and Kerning Check running, [KERNING.md](KERNING.md). Auto spacing, a sister script: running, [SPACING.md](SPACING.md).
3. Automated kerning pairs.
4. Automated diacritic variants: running, [DIACRITICS.md](DIACRITICS.md).
5. Automated discretionary and ligature feature code: running, with alternates that take turns, [FEATURE-CODE.md](FEATURE-CODE.md), and the features of a font's forms.
6. A font check (language coverage, vertical metrics, masters): running, [FONT-CHECK.md](FONT-CHECK.md).

What already exists for each, and what FreeLab would build: [FEATURE-SURVEY.md](FEATURE-SURVEY.md).

Reads and writes through an AI app belong to FreeLab MCP, the separate `freelab-mcp` repository, and are on its roadmap.
